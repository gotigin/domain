- **No sale of personal data.** We do not sell personal information or use
  gotigin.com visitor data for third-party advertising networks beyond
  optional first-party analytics (see Cookies).
- **Minimal collection** on the company site: request logs and what you

- **Product data** (POS books, SMS bodies, WhatsApp webhooks, chat keys) is
