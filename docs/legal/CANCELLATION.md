## The deal in plain terms

Paid plans and hosted periods are sold as a **prepaid term**: you buy access
for a stated period. That purchase is meant to **stand for that period**. We
do not treat mid-term cancellation or automatic refund as a normal product
feature.

**Future renewals are never forced.** If a plan renews, you may stop the next
renewal so you are not billed for a later period. That is separate from
undoing money already paid for the **current** period.

## Law first

Where the law of your country (or a payment network / Merchant of Record rule
that binds the sale) requires a cooling-off, withdrawal, or refund right, we
comply. This policy does not reduce rights you cannot waive by contract.

## Current prepaid term

Unless mandatory law requires otherwise:

- There is **no self-serve mid-term cancel** that unwinds the current paid
  period or triggers an automatic refund or proration.
- You keep the paid access for the remainder of the term you purchased
  (subject to suspension for abuse, non-payment where applicable, or legal
  necessity — see the applicable Terms).
- Store purchases (Google Play, Microsoft Store, and similar) follow that
  store’s refund rules for that channel when those rules apply to the buy.

## Renewals

Auto-renew, where offered, is optional for the **next** period. You may
decline or turn off renewal before the next charge so you are not committed
beyond the current term. Stopping renewal does **not** by itself create a
refund of fees already paid for the current term.

## Exceptions — contact support

If you are in hardship, believe you were charged in error, are dissatisfied,
or need an exceptional review for any reason, email
[support@gotigin.com](mailto:support@gotigin.com).

You will be **heard**. We review case by case. A reply is not a promise of
refund; goodwill decisions are discretionary unless the law or the payment
partner requires a particular outcome.

## Payment partners

Checkout may run through processors or Merchants of Record (for example
Stripe or Paddle). Their receipts, tax handling, and any consumer rules that
apply to that checkout also apply to that purchase.
