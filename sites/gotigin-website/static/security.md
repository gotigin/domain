# Gotigin security

_Last updated: 22 November 2026_

## Practices (company site)

- **HTTPS** for gotigin.com (Firebase Hosting / TLS)
- **Least privilege** for hosting and domain consoles
- **Separate brand policies** for product apps that handle messages, books,
  or tokens — see each brand’s privacy and security notes

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you found a security issue in gotigin.com or a Gotigin brand
product:

1. Email [support@gotigin.com](mailto:support@gotigin.com)
2. Subject: `Gotigin security report`
3. Include steps to reproduce, affected URL or app id, and impact

Please give us a reasonable time to investigate before public disclosure.
Do not include customer personal data in reports unless strictly necessary.

## What is out of scope

- Attacks against third-party services (Meta, Google, Microsoft, carriers)
- Social engineering of individual users
- Denial-of-service against shared infrastructure without prior coordination
