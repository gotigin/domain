# Gotigin cookie policy

_Last updated: 22 November 2026_

This policy explains how [gotigin.com](https://gotigin.com) uses cookies and
similar technologies. Privacy: [/privacy](/privacy).
Cancellation: [/cancellation](/cancellation).

## What we use

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Strictly necessary** | Hosting / CDN session or load tokens | Deliver the site securely |
| **Analytics (optional)** | Firebase / Google Analytics cookies or local storage, if enabled on a deploy | Aggregate traffic and page performance |

We do **not** use third-party advertising cookies on gotigin.com.

## Analytics

When Analytics is enabled, Firebase Analytics / Google Analytics may set
identifiers to measure visits. You can block analytics cookies via browser
settings or extensions. Disabling analytics does not affect reading legal
pages.

## Managing cookies

Use your browser’s cookie and site-data controls to clear or block cookies
for gotigin.com. Strictly necessary cookies may be required for the site to
load correctly through our host.
