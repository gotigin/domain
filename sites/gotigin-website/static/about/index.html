What Gotigin is

Gotigin is the house mark and public face of GOTIGIN SOFTWARE & HARDWARE PRIVATE LIMITED. We build software and hardware products for businesses and communities — local-first business operating systems, messaging tools, and communication products — and we publish company policies here for stores, platforms, and partners that verify our identity.
