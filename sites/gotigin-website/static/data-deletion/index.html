Gotigin data deletion

Last updated: 20 November 2026

How to ask GOTIGIN SOFTWARE & HARDWARE PRIVATE LIMITED to delete personal data it holds about you. Privacy policy: gotigin.com/privacy.

Company website & support mail

gotigin.com does not create user accounts. To request deletion of personal data you sent us by email or form:

  1. Email support@gotigin.com
  2. Subject: Gotigin data deletion
  3. Include enough detail to find your records (date of contact, email used, topic)

We will delete or anonymise personal data we hold for that request within 30 days, except records we must keep for legal / abuse prevention (minimal, time-limited). We confirm by email reply when it is done.

Product apps & services

Each product has its own deletion steps:

Brand Products How to delete
Kumkaj Khaynjo, Moklanjo, Kirano, Tabiyat Your books stay on your own devices. Delete them in the app (Settings → Backup → Delete local data) or uninstall the app. For a product website account, email us.
Wadhu SMS Gateway, SMS Relay, Wadhu Connect wadhu.com/data-deletion
Ghal Bol Ghal Bol ghalbol.com/data-deletion

If a product has no deletion page of its own, use the same email and subject as above and name the product in the body.
